Our Repair Services

We repair and maintain a wide variety of domestic appliances with expert diagnosis and quality service.

Washing Machine Repairs

Fixing drainage faults, drum spinning issues, door seal leaks, electrical faults, and noise issues on all major models.

Models serviced include AEG, Beko, Bosch, Hotpoint & more

Cooker, Oven & Hob Repairs

Heating element replacements, thermostat fixes, door hinge repairs, and fan fixes for freestanding and built-in cookers.

Zanussi, Beko, Indesit, Belling & all leading brands

Tumble Dryer Repairs

Vented, condenser, and heat pump dryers. Resolving heating failures, belt snapping, drum stopping, and sensor issues.

Fast fault diagnostic & thermal safety checks

Dishwasher Repairs

Clearing blockages, replacing spray arms, fixing water heating problems, door latches, and pump replacements.

Integrated & freestanding models catered for

Fault Diagnosis & Servicing

Accurate fault finding to give you honest advice on repair vs replacement, saving you time and extra expense.

Professional & transparent assessments

General Domestic Appliance Maintenance

Routine checkups, safety checks, and part replacements to extend the operational life of your home appliances.
