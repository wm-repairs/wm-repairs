Washing Machine Repairs

A washing machine fault can interrupt the whole household. These checks can help you describe the symptoms before deciding whether to arrange a repair. They cannot confirm the exact cause remotely.

Simple checks to try first

Only carry out checks that are user-accessible and described in your appliance manual. Do not dismantle the appliance.

The machine will not start

Check that the socket works with another suitable appliance, the plug is fully inserted, the door is closed and the programme has been started correctly. Do not use damaged plugs or sockets.

Water is not draining

Check that the drain hose is not visibly kinked and that the household sink or waste connection is not blocked. Only inspect or clean an accessible pump filter if the manufacturer explains how to do so; isolate the machine first and be ready for water to escape.

The machine will not spin

Check whether the load is very small, heavy or unevenly distributed. Try redistributing the laundry and selecting the appropriate spin programme.

The machine is vibrating or noisy

Check that the appliance is level and that the load is balanced. Stop the cycle if there is loud banging, grinding or a burning smell.

Common washing machine problems

These are possible causes, not a confirmed diagnosis. Several different faults can produce similar symptoms.

Not draining

A blocked filter, obstructed waste hose or drain-pump fault may prevent the water from emptying.

Not spinning

An unbalanced load, door-lock problem, drainage issue or motor/control fault may stop the spin cycle.

Not heating

A heating element, temperature sensor or control issue may be involved.

Leaking water

Check for obvious water around the door and visible hose connections. A damaged seal, hose or internal component may need attention.

Stopping mid-cycle

The machine may be responding to a water supply, drainage, heating, door-lock or electronic fault.

Important safety advice

Stop the programme and isolate the machine if you notice smoke, a burning smell, sparks, repeated tripping of the electrics or significant leaking near electrical parts. Do not remove panels or attempt electrical repairs.

Never remove covers, handle exposed wiring or attempt repairs on live electrical equipment. For gas appliances, use a Gas Safe registered engineer.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my washing machine full of water?

It may not be draining correctly. A blocked filter, waste hose or faulty drain pump are possible causes. Check only accessible parts as described in the appliance manual.

Why does my washing machine not spin?

An unbalanced load or a drainage, door-lock or motor-related fault can prevent spinning. Note any error code and whether the drum turns during a wash.

Should I keep restarting it?

No. Repeatedly restarting a machine that is leaking, tripping the electrics or making severe noises can make the situation worse. Stop and arrange advice.
