Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our appliance repair services.

Do you charge a call-out fee? +
We charge a competitive call-out fee, which may vary depending on your location and appliance. This will generally be between £45 and £65. In many cases, if we can complete the repair without additional parts, there will be no extra charges.
Where are you based? +
We are based in Hagley, Worcestershire, and cover the majority of the West Midlands, generally within a 30-mile radius of Birmingham.
How quickly can you come out? +
We aim to attend as quickly as possible. Availability depends on the areas we are covering that day, but in most cases we can visit the same day or the following day.
Do you repair gas appliances? +
We currently repair electric appliances only, for which we are fully qualified and insured.
