Tumble Dryer Repairs

If your tumble dryer is taking too long, not heating or has stopped turning, a few simple checks may help identify the symptoms. The exact cause needs proper diagnosis.

Simple checks to try first

Only carry out checks that are user-accessible and described in your appliance manual. Do not dismantle the appliance.

Clothes are still damp

Clean the lint filter as described in the manual. Check that the load is not too large and that the selected programme is suitable.

The dryer is not heating

Check the selected programme and make sure any required water container or condenser maintenance has been completed according to the manual.

The dryer stops during a cycle

Check for a full water container, blocked filter warning or ventilation message. Allow the appliance to cool before checking user-accessible filters.

The drum will not turn

Stop the appliance. A drive belt, motor, drum support or control fault may need an engineer; do not remove covers.

Common tumble dryer problems

These are possible causes, not a confirmed diagnosis. Several different faults can produce similar symptoms.

No heat

Possible causes include a heating element, thermostat, thermal cut-out, sensor or control fault.

Slow drying

Restricted airflow, a dirty filter, condenser maintenance issue, overloaded drum or sensor fault may be responsible.

Drum not turning

A worn or broken belt, motor or drum component may prevent rotation.

Unusual noise

Worn rollers, bearings, drum supports or an object caught in the drum may cause noise.

Overheating

Restricted airflow or a temperature-control fault may cause excessive heat. Stop using the appliance if it becomes unusually hot.

Important safety advice

If you smell burning, see smoke, notice sparks or the appliance repeatedly trips the electrics, switch it off and stop using it. Never operate a tumble dryer with damaged filters or blocked airflow.

Never remove covers, handle exposed wiring or attempt repairs on live electrical equipment. For gas appliances, use a Gas Safe registered engineer.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my tumble dryer running but not heating?

A heating element, thermostat, thermal cut-out, sensor or control issue may be involved. Check the programme and user-maintenance warnings first.

Why does my tumble dryer take so long?

A clogged lint filter, restricted airflow, an overloaded drum or a condenser maintenance issue can reduce drying performance.

Can I keep using it if it smells hot?

Stop using it if there is a burning smell or unusual overheating. Have it checked before using it again.
