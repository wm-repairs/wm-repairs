Electric Oven Repairs

An oven that will not heat properly can have several possible causes. These non-invasive checks may help narrow down what is happening before you contact WM Repairs.

Simple checks to try first

Only carry out checks that are user-accessible and described in your appliance manual. Do not dismantle the appliance.

The oven will not heat

Check the clock/timer display, selected function and temperature setting. Some ovens will not operate until the clock has been set after a power cut.

Food cooks unevenly

Check that the correct shelf position and function are selected and that cookware is not blocking airflow.

The fan seems noisy

Switch the oven off and allow it to cool. Note whether the noise occurs during cooking or after switching off; do not reach behind guards.

The display shows an error

Write down the full code and model number. The manufacturer’s manual may explain what the code means.

Common electric oven problems

These are possible causes, not a confirmed diagnosis. Several different faults can produce similar symptoms.

Not heating

A failed heating element, thermostat, temperature sensor, selector or electronic control may be responsible.

Fan not working

The fan motor, wiring or control system may need inspection.

Grill not heating

The grill element or its control circuit may have failed.

Temperature inaccurate

A sensor, thermostat or control fault can cause the oven to run too hot or too cool.

Tripping the electrics

An insulation or electrical component fault may be present and should be investigated by a qualified person.

Important safety advice

If the oven trips the electrics, sparks, smokes unexpectedly or smells of burning, switch it off at the cooker isolator if safe and do not use it until checked. Do not remove the oven casing or test live electrical parts.

Never remove covers, handle exposed wiring or attempt repairs on live electrical equipment. For gas appliances, use a Gas Safe registered engineer.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my electric oven not getting hot?

The selected function, clock setting, heating element, temperature sensor or control system could be responsible.

Why does my oven trip the electricity?

A faulty element or another electrical insulation fault may cause tripping. Stop using it and arrange a professional inspection.

Why is my oven cooking unevenly?

Incorrect settings, blocked airflow or a fan, element or temperature-control fault may affect cooking.
